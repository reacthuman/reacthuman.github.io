35.9%of Dodge-scene decisions are unsafe

Every model, 23–66%. Most mistakes are freezing in place.

6 of 7models always prefer to retreat

Claude is the mirror image: catches well, engages hazards it should avoid.

27Bopen-weight model is the safest

Gemma-3-27B tops safety (88.2%); the flagship Claude Opus 4.8 is the least safe (64.7%).

0.48 mmedian miss even when the action is correct

Models reach with the arms but do not step forward. A multiple-choice benchmark would mark all of these right.

2×more freezing on slow hazards (42% vs 21%)

Speed is read as moving-or-not; a slow hazard is treated as no event.

0 / 280decisions questioned an object's material or weight

Foam anvils are dodged, steel apples are caught. Seeing is believing.
